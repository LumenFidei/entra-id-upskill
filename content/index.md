## The Four Exam Domains

- **User Identities** (20–25%) — tenant configuration, identities, external users, hybrid identity
- **Authentication and Access** (25–30%) — auth methods, Conditional Access, Identity Protection, Global Secure Access
- **Workload Identities** (20–25%) — app registrations, enterprise applications, managed identities, Defender for Cloud Apps
- **Identity Governance** (20–25%) — entitlement management, access reviews, PIM, monitoring and logs
